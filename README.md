# bloop
Bloop is a small desk pet that lives on a big colour screen connected to an ESP32. He has a cute face that blinks, looks around and reacts when you press his buttons, and he falls asleep when nobody talks to him. He is also useful: he shows the time, works as a study timer, tells the weather and air quality, reminds you to drink water, counts your study streak, shows messages sent from your phone, and even helps you make small decisions.
He also has a built-in Flappy Bird game.
# buttons 
POWER — Bloop on/off
PLAY — Pomodoro timer (short press) / mark study streak (long press)
WEATHER — Weather + air quality card
ORACLE — Decision helper
GAMES — Flappy Bird
